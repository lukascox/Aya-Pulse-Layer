THE session's main finding. Custom fan curve ran for the first time (three
prior sessions had the Fan card on SMART and produced no curve data at all).
The loop works. The curve as configured does almost nothing.

Across 528 fan-loop decisions on B spanning 38-76 C, the curve returned
target=20 in 527 of them and target=24 once. 20 is FanCurve.MIN_PERCENT, the
vendor-safe floor. W is identical: 530/530 at target=20.

The single non-floor sample proves the curve IS being evaluated rather than
falling back to a constant -- so this is a configuration, not a bug:

2026-08-02 15:42:24 PulseFan: CUSTOM fan running (autoTdp=false smart=true temp=76 applied=20% target=24)

For comparison, FanCurve.DEFAULT ((30,40),(50,50),(80,80),(90,100)) would
return ~76% at 76 C. The configured curve returns 24%.

Typical steady state, at 65-67 C, still at the floor:

2026-08-02 15:54:17 PulseFan: CUSTOM fan running (autoTdp=false smart=true temp=65 applied=20% target=20)
2026-08-02 15:58:04 PulseFan: CUSTOM fan running (autoTdp=true smart=true temp=67 applied=20% target=20)

CONSEQUENCE, and this is the part that matters: 20% of 255 is duty 51, and
the vendor's own idle point is duty 76 (30%). cap_poll confirms both values
present. So while the Custom curve is engaged, PULSE holds the fan SLOWER
than the vendor would at idle -- for the whole session, at any temperature
up to 76 C. The device is quieter and hotter than it would be with PULSE's
fan control switched off entirely.

The idle handoff works correctly and is unrelated:
2026-08-02 15:00:07 PulseFan: CUSTOM idle -> vendor Smart (target=20 floor=20 temp=39 idle=1)
